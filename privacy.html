The short version

We collect only what the site needs to work. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with any person, company, or other entity for their own use. The only time we will disclose it is to law enforcement, for a lawful purpose, when we are legally compelled to do so.

Who we are

This site is run by the Taylor Lake Estates lake association for people who live in the neighborhood. The lake manager administers it. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL OR MAILING ADDRESS], or the “Contact the lake manager” section of the site once you have an account.

We do not collect payment card numbers. Card payments are made on Stripe’s own payment page, and your card details go to Stripe, never to this site.

Photos are resized in your browser before they upload. That step removes the hidden data cameras attach to a photo, including any GPS location.

The site has no advertising and no analytics or visitor tracking.

Who can see what

  • Anyone visiting: the lake status, weather, and the guides. No personal information.
  • Signed-in residents: the display names of other residents on their catches, photos, and board posts. Residents never see each other’s email or street addresses.
  • Lakefront members: the above, plus members-only notices and vote totals. They do not see how other properties voted.
  • The lake manager: account requests (name, email, address), problem reports, messages sent to the manager, the dues ledger, and how each property voted.

How we use it

To run the site: approve accounts, sign you in, show posts to residents, keep the dues ledger, count votes, and answer your messages. We do not use it for marketing.

Sharing

We never sell, rent, or trade your information, and we do not give it to any person, company, or other entity for their own purposes.

We will disclose information to law enforcement only for a lawful purpose and only when we are compelled to, for example by a subpoena, court order, or warrant. We will disclose no more than is required.

The site runs on a small number of service providers that store or carry data on our behalf. They act on our instructions and are not given your information for their own use:

  • Supabase stores the database, the sign-in system, and uploaded photos.
  • Fly.io hosts the web pages.
  • Stripe processes card payments for dues. Stripe receives what you enter on its payment page under its own privacy policy.
  • Open-Meteo supplies the weather. Your browser asks it for the forecast for the lake, so it sees your device’s IP address but nothing about your account.
  • [EMAIL DELIVERY SERVICE] delivers sign-in emails.

Cookies and browser storage

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. It stores three things in your browser’s local storage:

  • your sign-in session, so you stay signed in;
  • a note that you have seen the storage notice;
  • the latest weather reading, for up to 15 minutes, so the page loads faster.

You can clear these at any time in your browser settings. Clearing them signs you out. Stripe may set its own cookies on its payment page.

How long we keep it

We keep account information while your account is active. Posts stay until you or the lake manager remove them. Dues and voting records are association records and are kept for [RETENTION PERIOD]. Declined account requests are deleted after [RETENTION PERIOD].

Your choices

You can remove your own catches, photos, and board posts at any time. To see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, or to close your account, contact the lake manager. We will act on your request within [NUMBER] days, except for records the association is required to keep.

Children

Accounts are for adults who live in the neighborhood. The site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Connections to the site are encrypted. Access to personal information is limited by account type, and there are no passwords to steal because sign-in is by emailed link. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your information, we will tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your information is used, we will post a notice on the site before the change takes effect.

1. Who may use the site

Anyone may read the public pages. Accounts are for people aged 18 or older who live in Taylor Lake Estates. The lake manager approves each account after checking the address given. Dues and voting are for lakefront members of the association.

2. Your account

You sign in with a link sent to your email address. Keep that email account secure, because anyone who can read it can sign in as you. Give accurate information when you request an account. One person, one account. Tell the lake manager if you move out of the neighborhood.

3. What you post

You keep ownership of what you post. By posting, you give the association permission to display it on this site to the people it is meant for. Post only photos you took or have the right to share, and leave out people who have not agreed to be pictured.

Do not post anything unlawful, harassing, threatening, or obscene, anyone else’s private information, or advertising other than neighbor-to-neighbor sale items on the board.

4. Moderation

The lake manager may remove any post and may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms.

5. Dues payments

Card payments for dues are processed by Stripe. Dues amounts, due dates, late charges, and refunds are set by the association, not by this site. A payment shows as paid on the site once the lake manager confirms it.

6. Votes

Each lakefront property has one vote, cast by a signed-in lakefront member of that household. A vote cannot be changed once cast. Whether a vote taken on this site is binding or advisory is decided by [THE ASSOCIATION’S BYLAWS OR BOARD].

7. Lake information and safety

Lake status, weather, moon phases, and the fishing outlook are provided for general information. They may be late, incomplete, or wrong. The depth map is illustrative and was not surveyed. Nothing on this site tells you that the water, the ice, or the weather is safe. You use the lake at your own risk and are responsible for your own safety and that of your guests and children.
